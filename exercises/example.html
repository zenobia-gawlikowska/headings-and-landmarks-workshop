Summer Community Workshops 2026

20–21 June · Riverside Library Learning Centre · Free admission

About the workshops

Join local artists, makers and volunteers for a weekend of hands-on workshops designed to help people learn new skills and connect with others in the community. Activities are suitable for beginners and experienced participants alike.

All sessions are free to attend. Some workshops have limited places and require advance registration at the information desk or by phone.

Workshop schedule

Saturday 20 June

  • 10:00 · Studio Room Introduction to watercolour painting
  • 12:30 · Learning Centre Community gardening and urban growing tips
  • 15:00 · Room 2 Beginner photography workshop (registration required)

Sunday 21 June

  • 11:00 · Studio Room Creative journaling and sketchbook techniques
  • 13:00 · Learning Centre Digital skills session for everyday life
  • 16:00 · Café Community showcase and networking event
Venue information

Riverside Library is located beside Central Park and can be reached by bus routes 4 and 12. Accessible parking spaces are available near the main entrance.

Accessible workstations are available throughout the venue. Assistive listening systems can be provided for selected sessions upon request.
